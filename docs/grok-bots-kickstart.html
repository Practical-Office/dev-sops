Mental model

Five layers

Every workflow sits on the same stack. Learn the layers once; apply them to any bot lane on your map.

LayerWhat it isYour job
BotThe agent that receives your brief and produces outputPick the right bot for the lane; know its limits
Shared computerThe environment where the bot runs — files, browser, terminalConfirm access before you brief; verify what the bot touched
Skills / routines / connectorsReusable capabilities the bot invokes (APIs, MCP, scheduled jobs)Match the brief to available skills; name what must connect
HandoffsStructured transfer between bot, human, and Monday ownerWrite the handoff; name who owns what after class
ApprovalsHuman gates before irreversible or sensitive actionsPause at money, legal, send, delete, and client PII — escalate when needed
